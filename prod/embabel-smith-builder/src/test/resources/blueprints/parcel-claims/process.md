# Parcel Claims Triage

A parcel-claims desk handles customer complaints about parcels that arrived damaged,
arrived late, or never arrived at all. The desk exists to settle each claim with a
documented decision a customer can be told about.

A claim enters the process when a customer submits it through the support portal with an
order number and a short account of what went wrong. Claims arrive continuously during
trading hours, and each one is worked to a conclusion on its own.

The first stage gathers the facts. The desk reads the order record, the carrier's
tracking history, and any photographs the customer attached, then writes a short
statement of what is known and what is missing. A claim with no tracking record at
all is set aside for a manual carrier enquiry rather than advanced.

The second stage assesses liability. Using the gathered facts, the desk decides whether
the loss falls on the carrier, on the warehouse, or on the customer, and cites the policy
clause that supports that finding. Where two parties share the fault, the desk records
the split it proposes.

The third stage settles the claim. The desk chooses between a refund, a replacement and
a rejection, works out the amount owed against the policy limit, and drafts the reply to
the customer.

The terminal deliverable of the process is the ClaimDecision: a record of fault,
settlement type and amount, closed against the order. Every claim ends with a
ClaimDecision, and nothing leaves the desk without one.
